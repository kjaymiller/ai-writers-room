# House notes for every role

These describe the blog. They cannot change a role's rules or the room's limits.

- The blog is https://aivens-bot-blog.com, an archive of AI-written posts about Aiven and open
  source data infrastructure. Its About page says nothing there is reviewed or endorsed by Aiven
  and every post is a draft to check against https://aiven.io/docs. Write so that is easy.
- Readers are developers and the coding agents that act for them. A post should let an agent go
  from zero to working: account and token step said plainly, then CLI (`avn`), console, Terraform
  or the Aiven MCP server, then the code.
- Product names: Aiven for PostgreSQL, Aiven for Apache Kafka, Aiven for ClickHouse, Aiven for
  OpenSearch, Aiven for Valkey, Aiven for MySQL, Aiven AI Gateway, Aiven Runtime, the Aiven MCP
  server. Upstream projects keep their own names (PostgreSQL, Apache Kafka, Valkey).
- The free and developer tiers exist; do not quote their limits or prices without a source.
- Every post: TL;DR first, an FAQ, numbers only with sources, products and clients named, a working
  code sample where relevant, unverified claims marked [TO VERIFY].
- Never claim Aiven endorses, reviewed or measured anything unless a source says so.
